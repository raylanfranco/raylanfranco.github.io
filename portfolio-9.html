Parallel 8

Media Platform for Amateur Boxing

Parallel 8 Productions is a media-forward platform built for boxers who are hungry to be seen but often overlooked by traditional outlets. Spearheaded by Jonathan, the project's mission is to amplify untapped talent by capturing raw, cinematic footage of fighters in their element — training, sparring, and competing — and distributing that content across high-visibility social media channels.

Victory Rush brought this vision to life with a sleek, mobile-first frontend backed by a fully-integrated admin panel and content management system, allowing Jonathan to upload new reels, update fighter profiles, and publish event footage without touching code. Every reel, post, and submission is routed through a streamlined backend designed for ease, speed, and long-term scalability.

The site serves as a neutral playing field where skill speaks louder than status, helping local fighters build name recognition before they enter the pro circuit. Whether you're a prospect with something to prove or a fan looking for the next big name — Parallel 8 is the feed where legends are born.
